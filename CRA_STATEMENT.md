# Cyber Resilience Act (EU) 2024/2847 – Status Statement

POCO C++ Libraries are free and open-source software. The project is developed
collaboratively by volunteers and made publicly available under the
Boost Software Licence 1.0 at https://github.com/pocoproject/poco.

## 1. No commercial activity.

POCO C++ Libraries are developed and supplied outside the course of any 
commercial activity. No person or organisation monetises the project or 
its releases. In particular:

  - the software and its source code are provided free of charge, with no
    restrictions on access; 
  - no paid support, maintenance, licences, subscriptions or other services 
    are offered by or on behalf of the project; 
  - the project does not process personal data for any purpose
    other than the security, compatibility or interoperability of the
    software; 
  - and any donations received are used solely to cover the
    project's costs and are not made with a view to profit.

The project therefore does not consider itself to be making a product
available on the market as a "manufacturer" under the CRA.

## 2. Supporting organisations. 

The organisations listed at https://pocoproject.org support
the project by, for example, providing hosting, infrastructure,
funding, or employee time. None of them supplies the POCO C++ Libraries as a
product, sells or monetises it, or takes responsibility for it as a
manufacturer. [No organisation has the purpose of systematically providing 
sustained support for the project's development as a product intended for 
commercial activities, or ensures the project's viability. 
Accordingly, no organisation acts as an open-source
software steward for the project within the meaning of Article 3(14)
CRA. Each organisation's involvement does not affect the non-commercial
status of the project.

## 3. Contributors. 

Individuals and organisations who contribute code,
documentation or other material do so voluntarily. Unless they control
the project's releases, roadmap or governance, they take on no
responsibility for the project under the CRA.

## 4. Downstream use. 

Anyone who integrates the POCO C++ Libraries into a product
they place on the EU market in the course of a commercial activity is
solely responsible for that product's CRA compliance. This includes due
diligence on third-party components. The project welcomes reports of
vulnerabilities and fixes at on a voluntary, best-effort basis.

## 5. Licence. 

This statement does not change the licence. The software is
provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as set out in the
licence.

## 6. Review. 

This statement reflects the project's understanding of the
CRA and the Commission's guidance as of November 22, 2026. 
It is reviewed whenever the project's funding, governance or 
distribution model changes.
